Prism privacy policy

Version 1.4.0 · October 7, 2026

Prism — Themes for Notion customizes the appearance of the Notion web app. This policy describes the extension code distributed with version 1.4.0.

What stays on your device

Prism stores your selected theme, appearance adjustments, and one original background image or video in your browser's local extension storage. The image record includes its filename, dimensions, and source URL when imported from the web. The image record also stores a small derived palette: sampled colors, a representative hue, and a light/dark preference. Media decoding and color analysis happen entirely on your device, without sending pixels to an AI service or other analysis provider. The optional soft backdrop displays a blurred copy behind the complete original using the same temporary local media URL. Original files are not resized or recompressed; a small video thumbnail and sampled palette are stored separately. Animated images retain their frames, and videos loop silently. The unlimitedStorage permission allows original files above the default extension-storage quota, with a 20 MB source-file limit. The standalone web preview uses local IndexedDB storage. A temporary page-instance marker coordinates saves between open extension views. Prism does not use cloud sync.

Notion page access

Prism inspects page structure and rendered styles to apply a coordinated theme. It inserts CSS and a background element into the Notion webpage. It does not collect or transmit your Notion text, documents, credentials, or browsing history. It does not alter saved Notion content or other people's appearance settings. Imported images are rendered in the webpage as backgrounds; Prism itself does not upload them to a server.

Image links and network requests

When you import a public HTTPS image or page link, Prism asks for browser permission to access its source host. For Pinterest sources it can request Pinterest and its image-host domains. If an image is hosted elsewhere, another permission grant may be needed. Prism fetches the media or public metadata with credentials omitted and does not execute remote scripts. Supported video playlists are fetched only during import to select and save the highest-resolution available single-file video; playback then uses the local saved file. During an import, its read-only webRequest listener reads redirect destinations for its own download requests, so each destination can be validated and checked for permission before it is fetched. The listener is removed after the download step. Prism does not record unrelated browsing traffic. Those source providers receive the network request, including your IP address and requested URL, under their own privacy policies. Downloading an image does not send your Notion document contents to that provider.

No analytics or developer service

This build has no Prism account, telemetry, advertising, tracking pixels, developer backend, or remote executable code. Prism does not sell user data or send local preferences or uploaded files to its developer.

Your controls

You can pause Prism to restore Notion's original appearance while retaining your preferences. Remove the background image in Theme Studio to delete the stored image and its derived palette. Reset appearance resets preferences while retaining the selected image for reuse. You can revoke image-host access in your browser's extension settings. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage.

Contact and changes

For questions, use the developer contact or support link provided on the extension's store listing or by the person who supplied your copy. Future releases may update this policy to reflect changes in behavior. Review the policy supplied with the version you install.

Prism is independent of Notion, Google, Pinterest, and browser vendors.
